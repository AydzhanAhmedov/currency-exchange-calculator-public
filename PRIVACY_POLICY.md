# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 19, 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Currency Exchange Calculator" application ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information that may be associated with you when using our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1. Automatically Collected Information

Our application is designed to work completely offline and does not collect any personal information from users. We do not require registration, do not store user data, and do not use external services for analytics or tracking.

### 1.2. User-Entered Information

All data you enter into the application (such as amounts in euros or leva) is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted or stored outside of it. This data is used solely for performing real-time calculations and is automatically deleted when the application is closed.

### 1.3. Advertising

Our app may display banner advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These advertising networks may collect certain information automatically, including:

- Device information (such as device type, operating system)
- Advertising identifiers
- IP address
- General location information (country/region level only)

We do not have access to or control over the information collected by these advertising networks. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these advertising providers for more information about their data practices.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information that may be processed by the application is used solely for:

- Performing currency calculations between euros (EUR) and Bulgarian leva (BGN)
- Displaying calculation results on your device screen

We do not use this information for marketing, analytics, or other purposes.

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not share, sell, distribute, or otherwise provide the information processed by the application to third parties. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

## 4. Data Security

Although the application does not collect personal data, we are committed to protecting any information that may be processed:

- All data is processed locally on your device
- We do not store data on servers or cloud services
- The application does not require internet connection for its core functionality

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13 years of age, we will delete it immediately.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through application updates in the app store or through other appropriate means.
